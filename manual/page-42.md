# Vehicle care and care products

## Vehicle care and care products

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> Regular and competent care serves to maintain the value of the Pedelec and is a prerequisite for the recognition of warranty claims. Corrosion due to lack of care or due to winter operation is not subject to the warranty.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Rubber and plastic parts must not be damaged by aggressive or penetrating cleaning agents and solvents.

> [Figure: A spray/jet of water aimed at the bike, crossed out — high-pressure cleaning is prohibited.]

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> After cleaning or before riding, always carry out a brake test first!

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Do not use any steam or high-pressure cleaners! The high water pressure can cause damage to the bearing points, seals, the battery and the entire electrical system.

> [Figure: A hand with a soft sponge, a bucket of clean water and a wash mitt/leather cloth — the recommended cleaning method.]

## Cleaning

- For washing, use exclusively a soft sponge and clean water.
- Re-polish only with a soft cloth or leather.
- Do not wipe off dust and dirt with a dry cloth (scratches in the paintwork and on the trim parts).
