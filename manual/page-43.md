# Vehicle care and care products

## Vehicle care and care products

> [Figure: A spray bottle and several bottles/cans of care and preservation products.]

### Care products

The Pedelec should, when required, be cared for with commercially available preservation and care products.

- Treat corrosion-prone parts and, in winter, regularly with preservation or care products.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> For plastic parts, do not use any silicone-containing care products and paint-polishing agents.
> - Preserve frame and aluminium parts after longer journeys, grind, clean and treat with a commercially available corrosion-protection agent.

## Winter operation and corrosion protection

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> In the interest of environmental protection, we ask you to use preservation products sparingly and not to use them in this way; use only such products that are marked as environmentally friendly.

If the Pedelec is used in the winter months, considerable damage can occur due to road salt.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Do not use any warm water — reinforced salt action!
> - Clean the Pedelec immediately after the journey with cold water and grind.
> - Allow the Pedelec to dry well.
> - Treat corrosion-prone parts before and during operation with corrosion-protection agents on a wax base, and repeat several times where appropriate.

### Repairing paint damage

Repair small paint damage immediately with a suitable paint stick.

## Tyre care

> [Figure: A front wheel/tyre with the tread (1) labelled.]

When the Pedelec is not in use for a longer period of time, it is advisable to take the weight off the tyres, so that the tyres are not loaded on one side. Storing the tyres over a longer period in rooms that are too warm — such as a boiler room — is to be avoided.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> The tyre tread depth (1) must not fall below 1 mm. Driving over kerbstones, sharp-edged obstacles, potholes or stones can cause damage to the rim (spoke fracture) or to the tyre (carcass tear), whereby too low a tyre pressure is causally responsible for this. There is no warranty claim for this.
