# Inserting the battery / Charging the battery on the vehicle

## Inserting the battery

> [Figure: The battery being fitted into the frame's down tube. Callout 1 points to the battery, callout 2 to the lock with the key inserted; arrows show the battery being pushed into place.]

1. Battery
2. Lock/Key

- First insert the battery (1) at the bottom into the frame and push it in as far as it will go.
- Turn the key (2) to the left and withdraw it.

## Charging the battery on the vehicle

> [Figure: The charging plug (3) connected to the battery socket on the frame, with the key still shown in the battery lock.]

3. Charging plug

> [Figure: The DERUIZ charger (4) with its mains plug (5).]

4. Charger
5. Mains plug

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> The battery can also be charged while installed.
> - Connect the charging plug (3) of the charger (4) to the battery socket.
> - Connect the mains plug (5) of the charger to the power supply (100–230 V, 50–60 Hz).
> After approx. 3–5 seconds the charging process starts. The charger becomes slightly warm during charging.
