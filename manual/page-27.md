> **QUARTZ & QUARTZ M** — Operating Manual · Operation

# Adjusting saddle height · Adjusting the saddle

## Adjusting saddle height

> [Figure: Saddle and seatpost area, showing the quick-release lever and the "3 max." maximum-extension marking on the seatpost]
>
> 1. Quick-release lever
> 2. Adjusting nut
> 3. "3 max." — maximum extension marking on the seatpost

- Open the quick-release lever (1), determine the saddle height and clamp the quick release to close it.
- By turning the adjusting nut (2) on the quick-release lever, the clamping force can be regulated.
- The quick-release lever (1) must close with noticeable resistance (approx. 15 kg).

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
>
> A quick-release lever (1) that is not fully closed can open again. As a result, the saddle may twist while riding or slip downwards. This can lead to falls.
>
> Closing the quick-release lever must require so much force that the palm of your hand is needed for it. If the tension is then strong enough and the saddle can no longer be twisted with great force, it must not be turned any further.
>
> Make sure that the saddle is not pulled out beyond the marking (3).

## Adjusting the saddle

> [Figure: Top view of the saddle on the seatpost, with horizontal double arrows indicating the fore-and-aft adjustment; the clamp bolts (3) are marked on both sides]
>
> 3. Clamp bolts (saddle clamp)

The saddle can additionally be tilted and adjusted in the longitudinal direction.

- Loosen the bolts (3).
- Bring the saddle into the desired horizontal position, i.e. move it forwards or backwards, and tighten both bolts.

***Tightening torque 8 Nm.***

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
>
> To set your individual saddle tilt, first start from a strictly horizontal saddle position.
