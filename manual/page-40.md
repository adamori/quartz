# Removing the front wheel for transport

## Removing the front wheel for transport

> [Figure: Left — front wheel hub showing quick-release lever (1), front wheel/fork (3) and axle nut (2). Right — quick-release lever shown with positions A (open) and B (closed); the hub with quick release (1).]

### Removing:

- Open the quick-release lever (1) and slacken the axle nut (2) by a few turns.
- Take the front wheel (3) out of the fork downwards.

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> A quick-release that is not fully closed can open again on its own. This can lead to serious falls. Closing the quick-release lever must require a noticeable amount of force, so that the quick-release holds in the closed position. Only then is the tension strong enough. Approx. 15 kg.
>
> Position A = OPEN
> Position B = CLOSED

> [Figure: Right-hand pair — front hub and disc brake showing axle nut (2), fork dropout (4), brake disc (3), quick-release (1) and brake pads/caliper (5).]

### Installing:

- Insert the front wheel (3) into the dropouts (4) of the front fork.
- Take care that the brake pads (5) on the disc brake do not become jammed.
- Pre-tension the axle nut (2) and close the quick-release lever (1).
- The quick-release lever (1) must point upwards after closing, and close with noticeable counter-pressure.

**Carry out a functional check of the disc brake!**
