# Foreword

It is the user's responsibility to keep the Pedelec ready for operation, to maintain
its roadworthiness through inspection (and servicing), and to carry out regular routine
checks such as the seating of the bolts (tightening torque), tyre pressure and lighting.
Familiarity with this operating manual is therefore a prerequisite.

For safety reasons and for warranty purposes, please also be sure to observe the
information on modifications, accessories and spare parts.
