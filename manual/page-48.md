> **QUARTZ & QUARTZ M** — Operating Manual · Maintenance & Servicing

# Crank Arms, Pedals

## Crank Arms

> [Figure: View of the bottom-bracket/crank area of the frame. Callout 1 points to the crank arm.]

1. Crank arm

The crank arms (1) can become loose over the course of riding. Check regularly that the crank arms are seated firmly. If, when you move the crank arms vigorously sideways, no play can be felt, all is well. If you find that the crank arms or the bottom bracket are loose, please contact a Deruiz service partner.

The crank arms are tightened to a maximum of 35 Nm.

- Tighten from behind at the crank arm (1) using an Allen key.

## Pedals

> [Figure: Close-up of a pedal. Callout 2 points to the pedal.]

2. Pedal

Check regularly that the pedals (2) are firmly screwed into the crank arms. Note that the pedals are fitted with different threads:

- Left pedal with left-hand thread: It is screwed in anticlockwise (counter-clockwise).
- Right pedal with right-hand thread: It is screwed in clockwise.

On the pedals there is a corresponding marking on the flat of the spanner (wrench) flat: "L" for the left side and "R" for the right side.
