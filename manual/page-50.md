# Adjusting the Headlight

> [Figure: Close-up of the headlight mounted at the front of the bike. Callout 1 points to the headlight; callout 2 points to the adjustment screw.]

1. Headlight
2. Adjustment screw

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> The headlight (1) is fitted with a non-replaceable bulb (LED/diode light). Before it functions, check whether the cable connection is interrupted. If this is the case, the headlight must be replaced.

The headlight (1) is to be tilted forwards so that the centre of the light cone, at a distance of 4 m, lies only half as high as it does at the exit from the headlight.

> [Figure: Side view of the bike with a shaded light cone projected onto the ground, the beam reaching a distance marked "8 m".]

In practice, this means that an illumination of the road surface of at most 8 m is permissible.

By loosening the screw (2), the headlight can be tilted accordingly.
