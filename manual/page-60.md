> **QUARTZ & QUARTZ M** — Operating Manual · Warranty

# Warranty Policy

| No. | Component | Warranty period | Scope of warranty coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Motor | 24 months | The motor can be replaced if it cannot be started within 24 months due to an internal short circuit, coils, or phases. Damage caused by the inner wiring coming loose, by the consumption of magnets, or by the magnets coming loose, where the axle is deformed and other quality problems arise, cannot be repaired. The motor or axle may not be deformed in general. |
| 2 | Motor Hall sensor, motor bearing | 24 months | Hall sensor failure, loud bearing noises or cracks. |
| 3 | Lithium battery | 24 months | Battery leakage occurs, or the capacity is less than 60 % within the first 12 months. Further 12-month period applies to maintenance (excluding battery locks, battery housing, charging sockets, battery holders and other accessories). For other battery types, no separate specifications can be given. |
| 4 | Frame, handlebar, rear section, front and rear hub, cable, saddle, seatpost | 24 months | Performance defects due to product causes cannot be repaired. Welding, soldering, tearing, twisting and peeling of paint on the handlebar grip and the handlebar can be replaced. Damage caused by human influences such as impacts, overweight and unauthorised modifications is not covered. Changes to the type of cable cannot be undertaken. |
| 5 | Controller, charger, cable, switch, power management, switch, lock, blinker, horn, brake lever, switch, protective circuit, throttle, damper, mounts, pedal-arm fittings, crank-arm fitting, backpedal brakes, power-off switch | 24 months | Defects in performance can be remedied. Desoldering, soldering, tearing, twisting and peeling can be replaced. Damage caused by human influences such as impacts and unauthorised changes is not covered by the warranty. |
