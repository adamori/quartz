# Charger functions

> [Figure: The DERUIZ charger (black power supply unit) with mains cable and charging cable. Callout 1 points to the charge-indicator LED on the top of the unit.]

1. Charge indicator

- Charge indicator (1)

This charger is suitable for Li-Ion batteries. The LED (light-emitting diode 1) on the charger continuously informs you about the operating state. This way you are informed at a glance about the status and the charging progress of the battery.

| **Charge cycle and LED indicators** | |
|---|---|
| **LED** | **MODE** |
| Green | Battery not yet connected |
| Red | Charging in progress — current is flowing |
| Green | Charging complete — no current is flowing |

## Battery and charger: Handling

To ensure a long battery service life, observe the following information:

- Before charging, bring the battery to about room temperature. Leave the battery on the charger only when the Pedelec is at room temperature (in several days at the earliest).
- Store dry for at least 15 days at 20 °C.
- Charge only at room temperature.
- Store at 30 % to 60 % charge capacity.
- **Recharge every 2 months.**
- When putting the battery back into use, the battery should be left on the charger for about 1 day.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Failure to observe this can lead to deep discharge of the battery. No warranty can be accepted for a deeply discharged battery.
