# Safety information

## Safety information

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> The Pedelec (EPAC) is, like all mechanical assemblies, subject to wear and heavy stress. Different materials and components may react differently to wear or sustained loading. If the planned service life of a component is exceeded, this component may suddenly fail and possibly cause harm to the rider.
> Any kind of cracks, grooves or colour changes in highly stressed areas indicates the end of that component's service life; the component must then be replaced.

### Road safety

The E-bike is only suitable for journeys on paved roads and paths. Therefore do not ride off the paved paths or into off-road terrain. Use the E-bike neither for water crossings, for off-road jumps nor for sporting events. While riding you should bear in mind that, at an average speed of approx. 18 km/h, 5 metres per second are covered. Keep an appropriately sufficient distance from other road users.

Ride to suit your own ability. Do not ride your E-bike hands-free or alongside one another.

You must be able to perceive warning sounds. Therefore do not use any headphones in road traffic.

### Riding safety

For riding safety, the correct adjustment of the saddle height to suit body size, the prescribed tyre pressure and sufficient tyre tread, as well as the proper functioning of the brakes and lighting, are essential. After a few months a check of the fastening parts is necessary; before first use and every six months, check the headset bearings, handlebar, saddle, crank arms and pedals for firm seating, and have them re-tightened by a Deruiz partner if necessary.
