# Assembly and Commissioning

To ensure flawless operation of the Pedelec and its assemblies, observe, check and adjust the following points during assembly and commissioning:

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Be careful when cutting open the packaging with a knife, as vehicle parts underneath may be damaged! Sharp-edged metal clips may be present on the packaging!

- Remove the packaging.
- Inspect for visual defects, transport damage and completeness.
- Install the handlebar onto the stem and adjust.
- Install and connect the display.
- Check the handlebar controls, shifter, control unit and bell on the handlebar for secure fit.
- Adjust the seatpost with saddle and check for secure fit.
- Install the pedals.
- Install the headlight at the front.
- Check the brake and gear settings, adjust if necessary.

## Scope of Delivery

> [Figure: Line drawing of the front portion of the e-bike (front wheel and fork) alongside the included accessories — a battery charger, an open-end wrench, a set of Allen keys, and two pedals; the accessory group is labelled 1.]

**WICHTIG: CHECK THE BOLTS OF THE FRONT AND REAR WHEEL FOR SECURE FIT!**

- Check the braking function of the front and rear wheel brakes.
- Check the shifting function, readjust if necessary.
- Check all safety-relevant bolted connections of the wheels, brake system, fork, swingarm, frame, etc. for secure fit.
- Charge the battery fully.
- Check the tyre pressure (front and rear, 4,5 bar) and correct if necessary.
- Carry out a test ride focusing on roadworthiness and functional safety.

Check the running noise of the motor. Also check the display settings. After commissioning, also pay attention to the listed checks so that the vehicle remains roadworthy at all times. As the user, you are responsible for this.
