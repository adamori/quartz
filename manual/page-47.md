# Checking the Steering Bearing

> [Figure: Side view of the Pedelec with the front end highlighted. Callout 1 points to the steering bearing (headset); callout 2 points to the fork steerer tube.]

1. Steering bearing
2. Fork steerer tube

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> Observe the maintenance intervals. If you ride for a longer period with a loose steering bearing (1), a fracture of the fork steerer tube (2) cannot be ruled out. A fall resulting in serious injuries could be the consequence.

> [Figure: Detail of the handlebar/headset area. Callout 3 points to the left hand brake lever.]

3. Left hand brake lever (front-wheel brake)

**Checking:**

- With the left hand brake lever (3, front-wheel brake) pulled, move the Pedelec back and forth. If there is play in the steering bearing (1), it must be readjusted.

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> When the handlebar is turned, the front fork must not jam and must swivel easily in both directions.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> If you have any problems, please contact a Deruiz service partner.

# Checking the Spokes

> [Figure: Close-up of a wheel showing the spokes. Callout 1 points to the spokes.]

1. Spokes

A firm seating of the spokes (1) is important for the wheels to run without wobble. Have loose spokes re-tensioned in good time. A broken spoke, as well as the spoke opposite the broken one, must be replaced immediately, and the wheel must be fully re-trued and, if necessary, re-spoked. Spoke breakage and wheel imbalance are frequently caused by improper tensioning of the spokes.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> The replacement, tensioning or loosening of spokes may only be carried out by an authorised Deruiz service partner.
