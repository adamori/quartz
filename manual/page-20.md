# Switching the display on/off · Assist level

## Switching the display on/off

> [Figure: Handlebar control unit with display, showing three buttons on the left]
>
> 1. ON/OFF button (⏻)
> 2. UP button (+)
> 3. DOWN button (−)

**1 ON/OFF button**

Press and hold button (1) "⏻" for 1.5 seconds to switch the display and the drive system on or off. The display switches off after 5 minutes of standstill.

## Assist level

> [Figure: Display showing battery charge level, speedometer (mph/km/h), TRIP/ODO/MAX/POWER readout, and the assist-level indicator]
>
> 4. Assist-level indicator

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
>
> After switching on the display, the system always starts in **Level 0** (4).
> Briefly press the **button (3) – DOWN** or the **button (2) + UP** and select the desired assist level for the Pedelec.
>
> The display shows the various assist levels:
>
> - **0** — no assistance
> - **P** — walk assist up to 6 km/h
> - **1–5** — with assistance
