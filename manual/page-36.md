# Riding in Pedelec mode · Pulling away · Riding — braking

## Riding in Pedelec mode

When the assist drive is switched on, the motor takes over part of the drive power up to 25 km/h. From approx. 25 km/h the hub motor switches off; the Pedelec is then driven exclusively by muscle power.

## Pulling away

The Pedelec can be ridden either with the assist drive switched on or off. When the assist drive is switched off, you ride the Pedelec like a normal bicycle. As soon as you start to pedal, the electric motor activates and provides additional acceleration to the Pedelec.
