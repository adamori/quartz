# Walk assist

> [Figure: Side view of the Pedelec being pushed along, with a "no riding" symbol (crossed-out rider) and motion arrows indicating the bike is walked, not ridden]

> ❗ **CAUTION**
>
> When riding the Pedelec is dangerous — e.g. on steep inclines or in difficult terrain — the walk assist may be used, provided you are not sitting on the Pedelec.

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
>
> At a speed of approx. 6 km/h the walk assist switches off.

## Walk assist (6 km/h)

> [Figure: Handlebar control unit with display]
>
> 1. UP button (+)
> 2. Walk-assist indicator "P" on the display

Activation is only possible while the **button (1)** is pressed and held, when the display is switched on. The **"P"** symbol (2) appears on the display.

The walk assist is switched off by releasing the **button (1)**.
