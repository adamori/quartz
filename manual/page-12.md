# Pedal Installation

> [Figure: A series of line drawings showing pedal installation. The first shows the two pedals with their threaded spindles (1) pointing toward the viewer; the second and third show the left (L) and right (R) pedals being threaded into the crank arms; a 15 mm open-end wrench is used to tighten them.]

1. Threaded screw-in spindle of the pedals

- The screw-in threads (1) must be greased.
- Screw the left pedal (L) into the left crank arm by turning the pedal spindle anti-clockwise.
- Screw the right pedal (R) into the right crank arm by turning the pedal spindle clockwise.

**Using a 15 mm open-end wrench, tighten both pedals to 30 Nm.**
