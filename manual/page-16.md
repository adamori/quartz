# Switching the Battery On/Off · Buttons on the Display Control Unit

## Switching the Battery On/Off

> [Figure: Close-up line drawing of the battery mounted on the frame, showing its control buttons. Callout 1 points to the on/off button and callout 2 to the LED state-of-charge indicator.]

1. On/off button
2. LED (battery state indicator)

- Switch the battery on with button (1). The LED (2) then remains lit to show the operating state.
- Switch the battery off by pressing button (1).

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> The battery switches off automatically after approx. 2 hours of inactivity.

## Buttons on the Display Control Unit

> [Figure: Line drawing of the display control unit showing the three buttons on its housing. Callout 2 points to the upper button, callout 1 to the centre button and callout 3 to the lower button.]

1. **ON / OFF button** — Press button (1) for 1.5 seconds to switch the display and the drive system on or off. The display switches off automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
2. **+ / UP button**
3. **- / DOWN button**

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> Long press of the button combination "+" and "-": activate or exit the menu.
> Short press of the button combination "-" or "+": select a menu item.
