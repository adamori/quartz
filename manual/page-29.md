# Important safety information for the charger

Before putting the charger into operation, please read this safety information.

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> Keep the charger out of the reach of children. To avoid the risk of injury, charge only DERUIZ lithium-ion polymer (Li-Ion) batteries. Other types of battery may explode if they are charged. This can lead to personal injury and damage to property.
> The use of accessories or batteries that are not sold or recommended by us can lead to a fire hazard, electric shock or injury. Always avoid operating the device in a damp or wet environment.
> Avoid any ingress of water into the device. If liquid does get in: immediately disconnect the charger from the mains and take it to a local specialist workshop or an authorised partner for inspection.
> Make sure the device stands on a level, secure surface.
> When not in use, disconnect the device from the mains. When doing so, do not pull on the cable, in order to avoid damage to the cable and the resulting risk of electric shock. Make sure that the mains cable is rolled up after use.
> Do not operate the charger with a damaged cable or plug. Have it replaced immediately by a qualified specialist.
> Do not operate the charger after it has received a heavy blow, been dropped or otherwise damaged. Never dismantle the charger yourself. Faulty assembly can lead to electric shock or fire.
> To prevent electric shock, please disconnect the charger from the mains before cleaning it. Cleaning of the device should only be carried out with a dry cloth or rag. Never use oil, water or solvents.
> An extension cable should only be used if absolutely necessary. The use of extension cables that are not in good order can lead to fire or electric shock. If an extension cable must be used, then make sure that:
> - the number and shape of the pins of the plug exactly match those of the charger.
> - the extension cable is correctly wired and in a good electrical condition.
> - the cable cross-section is large enough for the current rating of the charger.
> - the extension cable shows no visible signs of damage.
> - when using a cable reel, the reel is fully unwound.
