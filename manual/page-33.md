# Removing the battery / Inserting the battery

## Removing the battery

> [Figure (left): Down tube of the frame with the battery (1) fitted. The lock (2) is at the top of the battery, with the key inserted into it.]

1. Battery
2. Lock

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Make sure that the battery does not fall down. It could be permanently damaged.

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> The battery (1) is secured with a lock (2).

> [Figure (centre): The battery (1) being released; an arrow shows it swinging out of the frame. Callout 3 points to the key/lock.]

1. Battery
3. Key

- Turn the battery (1) with the key (3) twice to unlock the lock.
- Remove the battery.

## Inserting the battery

> [Figure (right): The lower end of the battery being placed into the frame mount. Callout 4 points to the electronic contacts in the battery seat.]

4. Contacts

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Do not damage the contacts (4) of the electronics when inserting the battery.
