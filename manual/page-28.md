## Setting the suspension fork preload

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> Do not make any further modifications to the suspension fork.

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> The response behaviour of the suspension fork can be adjusted to suit the road conditions and your body weight.

The greater the preload, the more force is required before the suspension fork responds.

- The preload is set with the adjustment dial (1).

**PLUS + = firmer suspension:**
- Turning clockwise increases the preload.

**MINUS - = softer suspension:**
- Turning anticlockwise reduces the preload.

## Locking the suspension fork

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> Do not make any further modifications to the suspension fork.

- The lever (2) locks the suspension.
- Turn the lever (2) towards LOCK.
