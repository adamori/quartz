> **QUARTZ & QUARTZ M** — Operating Manual · Operation

# Charging the battery externally / Battery charge state

## Charging the battery externally

> [Figure: The battery in the frame with the lock at the top and the key inserted. Callout 1 points to the battery, callout 2 to the lock.]

1. Battery
2. Lock

- The battery (1) is secured with a lock (2).
- Unlock the battery and pull it out.

> [Figure: The DERUIZ charger with the charging plug (3) connected and the mains plug (5). Callout 4 points to the charger.]

3. Charging plug
4. Charger
5. Mains plug

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> - Connect the charging plug (3) of the charger (4) to the battery socket.
> - Connect the mains plug (5) of the charger to the power supply (100–230 V, 50–60 Hz).
> After approx. 3–5 seconds the charging process starts. The charger becomes slightly warm during charging.

## Battery charge state

> [Figure: The battery with its push-button and indicator LED. Callout 6 points to the button, callout 7 to the LED.]

6. Button
7. LED

Press the button (6). The LED (7) indicates the charge state. If the button is held down longer, the LED lights up blue, green and red briefly in succession.

| LED | Voltage |
|---|---|
| R red, flashing | U < 42.6 V |
| R red | 42.6 V ≤ U < 46.1 V |
| G green | 46.1 V ≤ U < 50.3 V |
| B blue | U ≥ 50.3 V |
