# Adjusting the handlebar and stem

> [Figure: Close-up of the handlebar clamp and stem area, with rotation arrows indicating handlebar tilt]
>
> 1. Clamp bolts (handlebar)
> 2. Handlebar position
> 3. Clamp bolt (stem)

## Adjusting the handlebar position

- Loosen the clamp bolts (1) with the 5 mm Allen key.
- Determine the handlebar position and tighten the handlebar with all clamp bolts (1).

***Tightening torque 6 Nm.***

## Adjusting the handlebar stem

- Loosen the clamp bolt (3) with the 5 mm Allen key by 1–2 turns.
- Set the angle position to approx. 30° of the handlebar stem and tighten the stem and clamp bolt.

***Tightening torque 12 Nm.***
