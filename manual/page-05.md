# Assembly and Commissioning

> [Figure: Two line drawings. Left: the rear of the bike with the front wheel positioned next to the rear wheel; a cable tie (1) secures the front wheel for transport. Right: the bike standing on its rear wheel and on an assembly stand (3), with a green arrow indicating it should be set down vertically.]

- Cut the cable tie of the front wheel.
- Carefully remove the front wheel (1), taking care of the left crank arm.

- Set the Pedelec down vertically onto the rear wheel (2) and the assembly stand (3).

> [Figure: Detailed line drawing of the assembly stand (3) clamped to the frame, supporting the bike upright.]
