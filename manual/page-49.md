# Changing the Tyre

> [Figure: Close-up of a wheel rim with two tyre levers inserted under the tyre bead. Callout 2 points to the tyre levers; callout 1 points to the valve.]

1. Valve
2. Tyre levers

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> In principle, always fit a new inner tube. For easier removal, plastic tyre levers should be used. You can obtain accessories with tyre levers and a puncture-repair kit from your specialist dealer.

If in doubt, please contact a Deruiz service partner.

## Removing the wheel

- Unscrew the dust cap from the valve. Push in the pin (1) and let the air escape.
- Push the long end of the tyre lever (2) under the tyre bead opposite the valve and hook it onto a nearby spoke. Insert the second tyre lever a few spokes further along and hook it in here as well. Release the tyre levers and run them along the rim. The tyre bead lifts over the rim edge on one side over the entire circumference.
- Pull the tube out of the tyre, starting at the valve.
- Check the tube and the inner surface of the tyre for damage and foreign bodies (nails, etc.).
- Insert the tube and pump it up just enough to give it shape, so that it cannot get pinched. Insert it into the tyre starting opposite the valve.
- Before fully inflating the tyre, push the tyre bead back over the rim edge by hand. Take care that the bead seats evenly all the way around at the same distance from the rim edge.
- Refit the wheel.

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> When inflating the tyres with compressor air-pressure devices (e.g. at petrol stations), caution is required. Due to the small volume of the tube, the maximum filling quantity is reached quickly. The tyres can burst.

## Checking the tyre pressure

Please note that the tyre pressure is always maintained. Non-compliance leads to poor road grip and premature tyre wear. The tyre pressure at the front and rear must not exceed 4.5 bar.
