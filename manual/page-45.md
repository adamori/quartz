# Technical Modifications, Accessories and Spare Parts

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> Technical modifications to the vehicle can result in the EC operating licence (type approval) becoming void.
> If technical modifications are to be carried out, our guidelines must be observed. This ensures that no damage occurs to the vehicle, that roadworthiness and operational safety are maintained, and that the modifications are permissible. The authorised specialist workshop carries out this work conscientiously.
> Before purchasing accessories and before making any technical modifications, you should always seek advice from an authorised specialist workshop.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> In your own interest, we recommend using only expressly approved original spare parts for Deruiz Bikes vehicles. The safety, suitability and reliability of these parts have been tested specifically for the Deruiz Bikes vehicle.
> For other parts, we cannot — even if in an individual case approval has been granted by an officially recognised technical inspection and monitoring body, or an official authorisation exists — provide any assessment, despite ongoing market observation, and we accept no liability in this respect.

You can obtain approved original Deruiz Bikes spare parts from a local specialist workshop or an authorised partner. There, the fitting will also be carried out professionally.
