# Function Overview

The display offers you a wide range of functions and indicators to meet your riding needs. The list of display contents is as follows:

- Battery capacity
- Real-time speed
- Distance data: ODO total distance, single trip, top speed, instantaneous power draw
- PAS assist levels
- Walk assist (6 km/h)
- Switch headlight on/off
- Wireless function (optional)
- Settings functions: reset single trip, backlight, speed unit, factory settings, wireless status and name (optional), automatic shut-off time and custom data display.
