# Safety and Maintenance Information

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> For safety reasons, you are prohibited from carrying out repairs and adjustment work on your own beyond a very limited scope. Improper work on safety-relevant parts endangers the rider and other road users. This applies in particular to work on the steering, brake system and lighting.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Before any work on the electrical system, the plug connection to the battery must be disconnected — including when replacing a bulb. Do not tamper with the electronics. Failure to observe this voids the warranty claim. Any work on the motor unit, the wiring harness, the battery and the charger, as well as their removal, leads to the warranty claims becoming void.

## Please note the following:

- Have maintenance work carried out during the warranty period — and also afterwards — exclusively by a recognised local specialist workshop or an authorised partner.
- Use only original Deruiz Bikes spare parts.

## Before every ride, the following points must also be checked:

- Check the chain and chain tension, readjust if necessary, clean it and lubricate it with chain spray or chain oil. Check the rear-wheel tracking and adjust if necessary.
- Check the steering bearing for play and for being too tight.
- Check the brakes for proper function; if necessary, lubricate the brake pads at the pivot points. (If braking performance is poor, check the condition of the hand brake lever, cable and brake lever, and readjust if necessary. Re-oil the joints and bearing points. Replace kinked or pinched cables. Oil-contaminated pads must be renewed — even the slightest oil contamination causes a reduction in braking performance.)
- Check the kickstand for proper function; if necessary, lubricate it at the joint.
- Check the wheels for lateral and radial run-out.
- Check the spoke tension.
- Check the tyre pressure regularly.
- Check the tyre tread depth.
- Check the lighting and signalling system; if necessary, adjust the headlight.
- Check the battery charge level.
