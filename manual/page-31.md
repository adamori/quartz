# Important safety information for the battery

> 🔥 **FIRE HAZARD**
>
> **There are many causes of fire**
> In this, it is not the product itself that is the problem, but rather improper handling.
> A lithium-ion battery generally reacts sensitively to improper handling. It is therefore important to exercise a certain amount of care when handling the battery.
> Among other things, manipulation — such as improper repair — can lead to such a reaction. A battery-external short circuit can occur, for example, when the battery is charged with the wrong or a defective charger. If the battery has been exposed to strong external heat, an internal short circuit can be the result.
> External influences, such as a fall or crushing of the battery, can damage the battery and make it prone to failure.
> For safety, batteries should not be charged near readily flammable materials. In addition, the battery should never be charged overnight or fully unattended. The charging location should be fitted with a smoke detector. If a battery does catch fire, it is important not to extinguish it with water, but rather to smother the fire with sand or blankets.

**Do not store in cold rooms**
As a matter of principle, we also recommend not storing batteries over a longer period in cold rooms. Anyone who wishes to use their Pedelec in winter or who does not use it for a longer period in an unheated garage should rather store the battery separately in a heated room.

In any case, the battery — like the Pedelec — should be stored, when not in use, in a dry and cool place and with a charge capacity of 30 to 60 percent.

Caution is also advised with deeply discharged batteries that have not been used for a longer time. Definitely never charge a battery that has remained discharged. The same applies to batteries that have fallen down or are damaged. Mechanical damage in particular can considerably increase the risk of explosion.

Damaged, defective or deeply discharged batteries can also cause an increased fire hazard through chemical reactions. Such batteries should therefore be taken for disposal in fireproof, dry containers or rooms.

DERUIZ accepts no liability whatsoever for such batteries that have not been properly stored.
