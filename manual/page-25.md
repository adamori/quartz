# Adjusting the seating position

> [Figure: Cyclist seated on the Pedelec, with an arrow indicating the saddle height; the rider's leg is shown nearly straight with the foot on the lower pedal]
>
> 1. Leg position when seated (not fully extended)

To set up an individual fit, you can adjust the saddle height, saddle position (fore and aft) and handlebar position.

Riding is particularly efficient when you set the saddle height as follows: when seated, your leg should not be fully extended (1); the heel must just be able to reach the pedal in its lowest position. The balls of your feet must be able to touch the ground. An excessively high seating position should be avoided.

> [Figure: Front of the Pedelec showing the handlebar/stem with rotation arrows, and the saddle/stem area marked]
>
> 2. Saddle and seating position (can be set lower)
> 3. Handlebar

For safety or comfort reasons you can also choose a lower saddle and seating position (2).

You can adjust the handlebar (3) as required.
