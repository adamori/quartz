# Important safety information for the battery

> [Figure: The DERUIZ frame battery shown from the side, with the LED charge-level indicator at one end.]

Important information that you should read carefully before commissioning and which we strongly recommend you observe:
- The Pedelec's batteries are charged to about 50% when new.
- Before first use, the battery must be charged for at least 24 hours.
- The battery reaches its maximum capacity after approx. 5 charge and discharge cycles.

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> The Pedelec's batteries and the charger are matched to each other. Therefore never charge the batteries with a different charger.

- Protect the battery from hard impacts and moisture.
- Before connecting the battery to the mains, check that the mains voltage matches the voltage stated on the charger's rating plate.
- A maximum battery service life is achieved if the battery is charged at an ambient temperature of between +10 °C and +30 °C.
- In the event of heavy use, the batteries can become warm. Always wait until the charging process is complete and the room temperature has been reached, or let them cool down for about 30 minutes.

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> - Keep the batteries out of the reach of children.
> - Never open or dismantle the battery yourself.
> - Do not allow any metallic objects to cause a short circuit on the battery.
> - Do not immerse in liquids.
> - A battery that has become unusable must under no circumstances be burned! There is a risk of explosion!

> ⚠️ **RISK OF CHEMICAL BURNS**
> - In the event of a leak and escape of electrolyte, do not use the battery any further.

> ➕ **FIRST AID**
> If electrolyte gets into the eye, rinse immediately for several minutes with clean water! Then consult a doctor without delay.
> If electrolyte splashes onto the skin or clothing, rinse off immediately with an acid neutraliser or soapy water. Do not neutralise with water alone. If acid has been swallowed, consult a doctor immediately.

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> A spent battery must be disposed of properly. Used or defective batteries must therefore be handed in for special-waste treatment. Please contact a DERUIZ service partner for this.
