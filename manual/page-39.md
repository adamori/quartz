# Safety information

## Safety information

- Ride defensively and adapt to the prevailing conditions.
- Do not ride under the influence of alcohol or medication, as these can impair your reaction capability.
- Hold the handlebar firmly with both hands. Only this way can you react to suddenly arising hazardous situations such as e.g. uneven ground.
- On sandy ground, foliage and wet roadways the tyres do not have such good grip on the ground as on dry asphalt. Take this into account when cornering and braking, in order not to slide away. Also bear in mind the longer braking distance.
- Switch down early on gradients.

## Recommendations on clothing

Many accidents happen because Pedelec riders are not recognised in time. Therefore it is sensible to wear bright and conspicuous clothing. Make sure that you do not wear wide clothing, which can get caught in the chain, on the handlebar, on the pedals or in the wheels. Always wear a helmet. Make sure that your helmet conforms to the SNELL and ANSI or to the standard. For the protection of your eyes, wear cycling glasses.
