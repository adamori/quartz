# Handlebar Installation

> [Figure: Four line drawings of the stem and handlebar clamp (bearing bridge). Top left: an Allen key loosening the 4 screws (1) of the bearing bridge (2); arrows indicate turning counter-clockwise. Top right and middle: the handlebar (3) being positioned centrally in the clamp; arrows (4) on the display cable connector show the two halves being brought together. Bottom right: the bearing bridge (2) being secured with the 4 screws (1) using an Allen key.]

1. Using the Allen key (4 mm), remove the 4 screws (1) of the bearing bridge (2) by turning counter-clockwise.

- Position the handlebar (3) centrally.

- Tighten the bearing bridge (2) with the 4 screws (1).
*Tightening torque 6 Nm.*

> ❗ **CAUTION**
> Make sure that the arrows (4) of the display cable face each other. Otherwise faulty contacts can occur and the Pedelec cannot be operated!
