# Front-Wheel Installation

> [Figure: Two line drawings. Left: the front wheel (1) being inserted into the dropouts (2) of the front fork, with the brake disc (3) sliding into the brake caliper. Right: close-up of the hub showing the quick-release axle (4) and the springs (5) being fitted on both sides.]

- Insert the front wheel (1) into the dropouts (2) of the front fork.
- While doing so, make sure that the brake pads (3) do not jam against the disc brake.

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> The springs (5) must be fitted with the slim side facing towards the hub.

- Pre-fit the quick-release axle (4) with springs (5) on both sides.
