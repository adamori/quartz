## Safety information

In addition, side-acting yellow rear reflectors are permitted, which may be located on the wheels (spoke reflectors). Further lights and direction indicators are not permitted.

Damaged or hidden safety-relevant parts, for example brake levers, frame, fork, handlebar or seatpost, must never be straightened, but exchanged for new ones. If there is a risk of breakage, in case of doubt turn to your **Deruiz partner**.

Carry out the following checks at regular intervals:

- Is the quick-release lever on the saddle firm? (see "Adjusting the saddle")
- Are the brakes working properly?
- Is the lighting system functioning?
- Is the tyre pressure correct? (see the "Technical Data" chapter)
- Is the lighting in order?
- Is the steering bearing and the headset bearing in order? (see the "Crank arms" chapter)
- Are the spokes firmly seated? (see the "Spokes" chapter)

Make sure there is no fall or accident with the Pedelec or that nothing is hidden or damaged (frame, handlebar, rims, etc.).

Is one of the named points not in order, you may not use the E-bike. The faults must be remedied immediately. If you cannot remedy them yourself, turn to a local specialist workshop or an authorised partner.

## Information for safe riding

If the Pedelec is in proper working order, it is ready to ride off. It then depends on your riding behaviour and the hazardous situations to avoid:

- Make yourself familiar with your Pedelec.

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> Before you ride off, hold the handlebar straight and firmly. For practice and familiarisation, switch off the motor and only switch on the motor while riding!

- Keep yourself to the traffic rules.
- Do not ride off the paved paths.
- Do not ride in the blind spot of other road users.
- Show your right of way, where you want to turn off.
- Bear in mind that, for other road users on bicycles, you can appear more rapidly than they expect; ride therefore with the other road users.
