# Front-Wheel Installation

> [Figure: Two line drawings. Left: the front wheel still in its cardboard packaging, with the retaining clip (1) at the hub. Right: the front fork with the assembly stand (2) attached at the dropout/pedal area; an arrow indicates pulling it off.]

- Remove the clip (1) from the front wheel and take off the cardboard packaging.

- Remove the assembly stand (2) from the front fork.

> [Figure: Line drawing showing the brake caliper (4) on the fork with the orange transport retainer (3) inserted between the brake pads.]

- Remove the transport retainer (3) from the brake caliper (4).
