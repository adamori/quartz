# Battery charge indicator

> [Figure: Display showing the various icons (assist, headlight, battery), speedometer in mph/km/h, TRIP/ODO/MAX/POWER readout and assist-level digit]
>
> 1. Battery charge (capacity) indicator

The capacity indicator (1) shows the charge state of the battery.

## Battery symbol and capacity

| Display | Capacity |
|---|---|
| Battery icon with 4 bars | 76% – 100% |
| Battery icon with 3 bars | 51% – 75% |
| Battery icon with 2 bars | 26% – 50% |
| Battery icon with 1 bar | 6% – 25% |
| Battery icon empty | 0% – 5% |
| Battery icon (outline only) | Flashing |

When the symbol (empty battery outline) flashes, the battery is almost empty. Charge the battery immediately.
