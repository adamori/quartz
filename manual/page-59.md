# Warranty Terms

A two-year limited warranty applies to all Deruiz electric bicycles from the date of purchase. Defective articles or parts will be replaced and shipped exclusively to the holder of the warranty. The warranty is not transferable to any person other than the original purchaser. All purchases must be made through direct Deruiz channels. The warranty only covers product defects that were already present at the time of handover.

Within 30 days of purchase, Deruiz will cover the costs for labour and parts that are connected with the warranty processing. It is the owner's responsibility to inspect the bicycle immediately upon receipt, at the latest within 3 days. Shipping complaints are time-critical. In order to resolve the problem or defect quickly, the owner must send an e-mail with a photo and/or video to **support@deruizebike.com**.

After the first 30 days, Deruiz will, within two years, supply replacement parts for defective articles free of charge. The owner is responsible for the shipping costs. It is necessary to send an e-mail with a photo and/or video to **support@deruizebike.com**, so that the warranty service can be carried out.

Deruiz reserves the right to require the proper provision of evidence/proof. This serves, not least, the safety of all riders. Acceptable evidence includes photos or videos describing the affected article or the problem. For the most up-to-date documents, bicycle models, additional information, instructional videos and more, visit us on the Deruiz e-bike channel.
