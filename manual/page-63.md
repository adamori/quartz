### Warranty claim

Within one month of purchasing the product, if a performance failure occurs, the product itself as well as the outer packaging must be complete and free of scratches. Products of the same model can be replaced. Excluded are damages caused by the user.

The product has a warranty of two years from the date of purchase. Lifetime maintenance (on a goodwill basis). Accessories are not covered by the warranty. Please refer to the previous pages for the exact warranty terms.

The warranty only applies to normal use.

All damage caused by the customer, self-disassembly, improper use or other external factors are not covered by the warranty.

Please keep this card available during the warranty period. If the card is not presented, or the card is altered without consent, the company reserves the right to further examine the matter for substantive proof.
