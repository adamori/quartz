> **QUARTZ & QUARTZ M** — Operating Manual · Assembly

# Front-Wheel Installation

> [Figure: Two line drawings. Left: the hub area with the axle nut (6) and the quick-release lever (7) being closed. Right: the quick-release lever shown in two positions next to the brake disc — Position B (open, lever pointing up/out) and Position A (closed, lever pressed down towards the disc brake).]

- Pre-tension the axle nut (6) and close the quick-release lever (7).
- The quick-release lever (7) must point upwards after closing and must close with noticeable counter-pressure.

Carry out a functional check of the disc brake!

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> A quick-release lever that is not fully closed can open again. This can lead to serious falls. Closing the quick-release lever must require enough force that the ball of the hand is needed for it. Only then is the tension sufficient (approx. 15 kg).
>
> Position A = OPEN
> Position B = CLOSED
