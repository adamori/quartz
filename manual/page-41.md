> **QUARTZ & QUARTZ M** — Operating Manual · Riding

# Transport · Carrying loads · Anti-theft lock

## Transporting the Pedelec with a motor vehicle

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> - The Pedelec may only be transported standing on its wheels and fastened on appropriate load surfaces (car, other transport vehicles, roof or rear carrier, trailer).
> - The weight of the Pedelec is higher than that of a bicycle. Handling and loading are made more difficult by this.
> - Observe the permissible total load of the carrier systems.
> - Before transporting your Pedelec, make sure that all parts that could come loose during transport are removed (e.g. air pump, panniers). The battery should be removed.
> - Rain can cause damage to the Pedelec. Avoid fast journeys in the rain.
> - Protect the control unit against moisture and cover it securely with a suitable protective cover.
> - Observe the regulations for journeys abroad. The marking for Pedelecs may differ.

## Carrying loads

> ⚠️ **WARNING**
> - No bulky loads to transport.
> - Do not cover the lighting.
> - Do not carry any persons.
> - Do not couple any trailer.

By carrying loads in any form, the handling of the vehicle changes. The larger the load, the more critical the riding behaviour becomes. As a basic principle, loads (e.g. shopping bags) should not be transported on the handlebar, but in the luggage compartment provided for it.

Child seats may only be used in accordance with the specifications of the respective child seat manufacturer. In Germany, children up to an age of 7 years may be transported in a child seat.

## Anti-theft lock

Secure your Pedelec with an additional cable lock against theft and attach it only to fixed structures such as lamp posts or fences.

The cable lock should secure both the frame and the rear wheel. Take care that the lock fits snugly against the Pedelec and the fixed structure.

**Lock the battery or, better still, remove it.**
