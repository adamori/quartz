# Headlight and Front-Mudguard Installation

> [Figure: Two line drawings. Left: an exploded view of the headlight bracket (1), mudguard-plate holder (2), washer (3) and screw (4) being assembled together. Right: the front wheel/fork with the mudguard (5) and its stays (6) being aligned.]

- Install the headlight bracket (1) together with the mudguard-plate holder (2), washer (3) and screw (4).
Tool: 4 mm Allen key

- Align the mudguard (5) with the stays (6).

> [Figure: Line drawing showing the mudguard stays at the fork dropout; an Allen key fits the screws (7) and washers (8) on both sides.]

- Install and tighten the screws (7) and washers (8) on both sides.
Tool: 4 mm Allen key

> ℹ️ **NOTE**
> Check the lighting system regularly, also after the first commissioning.
